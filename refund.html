Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

1. General Policy

Safi Digital Store provides digital tools and services. Because some services may begin processing immediately after a request or payment, refund eligibility may depend on the status of the service.

2. Cancellation Requests

Customers should contact Safi Digital Store as soon as possible if they wish to cancel a service request.

A cancellation may be considered when the requested service has not yet been started or processed.

3. Eligible Refund Requests

A refund request may be considered where a paid service could not be delivered due to a technical issue attributable to our service and the issue cannot reasonably be resolved.

4. Completed Digital Services

Refunds may not be available for digital services that have already been successfully completed or delivered, except where required by applicable law or where otherwise agreed by Safi Digital Store.

5. Duplicate Payments

If a customer is charged more than once for the same transaction due to a duplicate payment, the duplicate transaction may be reviewed for refund.

6. Service-Related Issues

If there is an issue with a delivered service, customers should contact us with details of the problem. Where reasonably possible, we may attempt to correct or re-process the service before considering a refund.

7. Refund Request Process

To request a refund, contact us using the information provided below and include:

  • Your name or identifying information used for the service
  • Service or product purchased
  • Transaction or payment reference, if available
  • Description of the issue

8. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, the refund may be processed through the applicable payment method or payment provider. The time required for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider or banking institution.

9. Third-Party Payment Providers

Where payments are processed through a third-party payment provider, additional payment-provider rules may apply to payment processing and refunds.
